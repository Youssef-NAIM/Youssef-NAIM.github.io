About Me

Automation & Mechatronics Engineer | Robotics

I hold a Master's degree in Automation and Robotics (Mechatronics track) from Clermont Auvergne University. I like owning a system end to end: understanding the specification, modelling it, sizing the hardware, writing the control and proving on the bench or on site that it works.

For my final-year internship at INRAE's TSCF research unit in Clermont-Ferrand, I worked on PhénoVigne: actively stabilizing a sensor mast mounted on a vineyard tractor. I built the CATIA V5 and MSC Adams models, sized the electric linear actuators and developed an IMU-based MATLAB/Simulink control law validated in Adams–Simulink co-simulation, reaching a tilt error below 1° against a ±2° requirement. Before that, at Axter Automation, I diagnosed and repaired an AGV on a customer site.
